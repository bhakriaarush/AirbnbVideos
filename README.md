## 📊 From Creative to Performance Marketing

Good creative is one part of advertising. A real campaign also needs a clear business objective, a suitable audience, an effective landing or booking journey, accurate tracking and honest reporting.

I'm building my understanding of how to evaluate ads with metrics such as:

- **CTR:** percentage of impressions that result in link clicks.
- **CPC:** average cost per link click.
- **Cost per lead/booking:** ad spend divided by leads/bookings.
- **ROAS:** attributed revenue divided by ad spend.

These are skills I'm learning and practising; they are not claims of completed campaign performance. See the [Meta Ads Practice Lab](./docs/meta-ads-practice-lab.md) for formulas, a sample test plan and reporting checklist.
