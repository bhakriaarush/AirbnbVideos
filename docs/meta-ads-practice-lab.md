# Meta Ads Practice Lab — Hospitality

This document records a practical learning framework for planning and evaluating hospitality advertisements. It is an educational planning resource, **not evidence that a paid campaign was launched**.

## 1. Start with the business goal

Choose one primary outcome before building a campaign:

- **Awareness/video views:** introduce the property and measure attention.
- **Traffic:** bring relevant people to a booking or information page.
- **Leads/messages:** generate genuine enquiries.
- **Bookings/sales:** optimize toward completed bookings only when the booking flow and tracking support it.

Do not choose an objective just because it sounds popular. Choose the one that matches the actual business goal and the available measurement setup.

## 2. Creative test plan

| Variant | Opening idea | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| A — Experience-led | Lead with the strongest atmosphere/experience shot | Emotional framing |
| B — Feature-led | Lead with a verified standout feature | Information priority |
| C — Itinerary-led | Show how a stay might fit a trip | Traveller use case |

Keep the offer, destination page, audience and budget consistent while testing a creative variable where practical. Do not make unsupported claims about amenities, distance, pricing or availability.

## 3. Simple measurement formulas

- **CTR (%)** = link clicks ÷ impressions × 100
- **CPC** = ad spend ÷ link clicks
- **CPM** = ad spend ÷ impressions × 1,000
- **Cost per lead** = ad spend ÷ leads
- **ROAS** = attributed revenue ÷ ad spend

Example practice calculation (hypothetical): if ₹1,000 in spend produces 20 purchases, cost per purchase is ₹1,000 ÷ 20 = **₹50**. This is a maths exercise, not a claim about an actual campaign.

## 4. Reporting checklist

Before interpreting results, record:

- Date range and campaign objective
- Amount spent and currency
- Impressions and reach (these are different)
- Video views and watch-time indicators, when relevant
- Link clicks and landing-page views, if available
- Leads, bookings and attributed revenue, if reliably tracked
- Attribution settings and any limitations
- What changed during the test

## 5. Good practice

- Obtain authorization before advertising a property on behalf of its owner.
- Use only creative assets and music you are permitted to use commercially.
- Verify prices, amenities, availability and promotional claims.
- Do not invent results or present practice data as client performance.
- Protect guest and customer data; use appropriate consent and privacy practices.
- Review platform policies and local requirements before launching.
