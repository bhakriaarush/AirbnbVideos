# Creative Concept: Viharika vs. Other Villas

> **Status:** Proposed creative concept — not a published ad or a claim about competitor properties.

## Goal

Explore a comparison-style creative that helps viewers understand what makes a particular stay appealing, using verified information and respectful, evidence-based messaging.

## Recommended angle

Instead of claiming that Viharika is universally “better” than other villas, focus on the experience the property can genuinely offer. A strong comparison communicates fit and differentiators without making unsupported claims about competitors.

## Suggested 30–35 second structure

| Time | Beat | Creative direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 sec | Hook | Open with the strongest available property visual and a short question about what matters in a stay. |
| 3–9 sec | Atmosphere | Show the property’s most inviting, verified visuals. |
| 9–16 sec | Standout features | Highlight only amenities and details confirmed by the property owner or an authorized source. |
| 16–24 sec | Experience | Show how the space could suit a getaway, group or occasion—without promising an experience that cannot be verified. |
| 24–30 sec | Proof and reassurance | Use accurate location, amenity or booking information if authorized and confirmed. |
| 30–35 sec | Call to action | Invite viewers to check availability or learn more through the official booking channel. |

## Hook options to test

- “Choosing a villa for your next getaway?”
- “What matters most in your next stay?”
- “A closer look at the Viharika experience.”

Treat these as draft lines. Confirm that the final video and property details support every statement.

## Fair-comparison checklist

Before using a direct competitor comparison:

- Identify the exact, relevant comparison criteria.
- Verify claims about every property from reliable, current sources.
- Avoid “best”, “cheapest”, “more luxurious” or similar superiority claims unless properly substantiated.
- Do not use competitor logos, photos or footage without appropriate rights.
- Obtain permission to advertise Viharika on behalf of the property.
- Use music and other assets cleared for the intended commercial use.

## Testing plan

Create two or three versions with the same overall objective and comparable conditions. Change one major creative variable at a time—for example, the opening hook or order of visuals. Evaluate attention, link clicks and genuine enquiries where reliable measurement is available.

No performance results are claimed in this concept document. Record real results only after an authorized campaign runs.
